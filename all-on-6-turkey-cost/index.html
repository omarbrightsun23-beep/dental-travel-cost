All-on-4 vs All-on-6

All-on-4 on this site is priced per arch (4 implants + 12 crowns). All-on-6 full mouth uses more implants per arch for higher stability, so the total is higher — but it is not simply double a per-arch All-on-4 price, because the brand and crown counts differ. Always confirm how many implants and crowns a quote actually includes.
