Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Travel

Real answers to key questions about overseas surgery, safety, and true travel costs.

How much does a full mouth of dental implants cost in Turkey including flights and hotel? ▼

A full mouth of dental implants (All-on-4 or All-on-6 per arch) in Turkey typically costs £4,800–£8,500 ($6,000–$11,000 USD) including surgery, premium titanium implants, 4-star hotel accommodation, and VIP clinic transfers. Adding round-trip airfare from London (£150–£300) brings the total all-in cost to roughly £5,100–£8,800, saving patients between 65% and 75% compared to private UK or US costs.

Why is dental work in Mexico so much cheaper than the US? ▼

Dental care in Mexico is 60% to 75% cheaper due to structural macroeconomic differences, not lower quality. Mexican dentists have significantly lower real estate overheads, lower staff payroll, and pay a fraction of the astronomical malpractice insurance premiums required in the US. In dental hubs like Los Algodones and Tijuana, high surgical volumes allow clinics to purchase identical FDA-approved implants (Straumann, Nobel Biocare) directly from manufacturers at wholesale prices.

Do you need two separate trips abroad for All-on-4 dental implants? ▼

Yes, true full-arch restorations require two separate visits spaced 3 to 6 months apart. Trip 1 (5–7 days) covers extractions, titanium implant placement, and immediate temporary prosthetics. Trip 2 (5–7 days) takes place after full osseointegration (bone fusion) to permanently screw in your final custom-milled zirconia bridge. Our calculator includes airfare and lodging for both trips to ensure complete budget accuracy.

How can I verify that an overseas dental clinic is safe and legitimate? ▼

Always verify that the clinic or hospital holds international accreditation such as JCI (Joint Commission International), ISO 9001, or GCR (Global Clinic Rating). Additionally, request written confirmation that the clinic uses globally recognized implant brands (such as Straumann, Nobel Biocare, or Zimmer) and provides a multi-year written warranty.

Can domestic dentists in the US/UK service or repair dental implants placed overseas? ▼

Yes, provided your international clinic issues you an official Implant Passport documenting the exact brand, platform diameter, and torque specifications. Since major international clinics use universal systems (Nobel Biocare, Straumann, Zimmer, Hiossen), any licensed prosthodontist in your home country can access matching drivers and replacement components.

How many days do I need to stay in the country after dental implant surgery? ▼

Oral surgeons recommend remaining in your destination city for at least 48 to 72 hours following surgical placement or bone grafting before boarding a pressurized commercial aircraft. A standard dental vacation itinerary spans 5 to 7 nights to allow proper post-op recovery and a final in-person checkup.

7-Point Patient Safety Checklist Before Traveling

1. Verify Accreditation: Confirm the clinic or hospital holds certification from JCI (Joint Commission International), ISO 9001, or GCR (Global Clinic Rating).

2. Check Implant Brand: Demand worldwide recognized implant brands (Straumann, Nobel Biocare, Zimmer, Hiossen) so any domestic dentist can service parts later.

3. Transparent Written Guarantee: Always secure a written warranty detailing what occurs if an implant fails to integrate or a veneer chips.

4. Surgeon Credentials: Ensure your surgeon is registered with their national dental board and has at least 5+ years of verified clinical experience in full-arch restorations.

5. Flight Clearance: Never fly immediately after major surgery. Follow the mandatory 48–72 hour ground rest period.
