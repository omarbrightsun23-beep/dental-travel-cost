About DentalTravelCost Index

An independent international procedural cost benchmark, hospital accreditation audit, and patient protection registry founded to eliminate deceptive pricing from global dental care.

The Problem: Misleading Partial Quotes in Overseas Healthcare

Every year, over 250,000 North American, British, and European patients travel abroad for dental surgery—drawn by advertised savings of 60% to 75% on procedures like All-on-4 dental implants, full-mouth reconstruction, and porcelain veneers.

However, traditional single-clinic marketing routinely advertises artificially low base surgical rates while concealing non-negotiable out-of-pocket costs: required bone grafting, custom titanium abutments, high-resolution 3D CBCT scans, 7 nights of recovery lodging, return international flights, and local VIP airport-to-clinic transfers.

Our Core Mission: We believe patients deserve radical pricing honesty before purchasing a plane ticket. DentalTravelCost delivers true all-in calculations that capture every single line-item expense—ensuring zero surprise bills when you arrive at the clinic.

The 4 Pillars of Benchmark Integrity

01

100% All-In Cost Accounting

We never report bare-bones surgical rates in isolation. Our engine models flight averages from top regional departure airports, 4-star recovery hotel stays, medication kits, local private clinic transfers, and currency exchange rates.

02

Zero Unbranded Fixtures

We disqualify clinics that deploy proprietary or unbranded white-label dental implants. Featured clinics must exclusively use genuine titanium implants from world-recognized manufacturers (Swiss Straumann Group, Nobel Biocare) with official warranty passports.

03

JCI & ISO 9001 Accreditations

Our clinical benchmark sources fee schedules and quality standards from hospitals accredited by Joint Commission International (JCI), ISO 9001:2015 sterilization certifications, and national health ministry surgical licenses.

04

Editorial & Data Independence

DentalTravelCost is not owned by any dental hospital or private equity clinic roll-up. Domestic benchmark fees are referenced against verified American Dental Association (ADA) Survey of Dental Fees and British Dental Association data.

Editorial & Medical Review Standards (E-E-A-T)

In alignment with Google Search Quality Rater Guidelines for Health & Financial (YMYL) content, all medical explanations, osseointegration timelines, and clinical safety checklists on this website are regularly reviewed by experienced healthcare economists and practicing clinical consultants.

We maintain strict separation between commercial operations and editorial evaluations. No clinic can pay to modify its procedural benchmark index or bypass accreditation checks.
