The First 72 Hours After Dental Surgery in Turkey: Food, Rest, Transfers and What to Avoid

By the DentalTravelCost team · Prices checked November 2026

The treatment itself is a few hours in a chair. What decides whether your Turkey trip feels manageable or miserable is the three days around it: how you arrive, what you eat, how you get between the hotel and the clinic, and when you fly home. This guide walks the first 72 hours after dental surgery in Turkey as they actually run, using the same stay lengths, transfer times and prices published across this site. Your clinic's own aftercare instructions always come first — this is the travel side of recovery, not medical advice.

First, an honest note: 72 hours is the recovery window, not the trip

Patients sometimes ask whether they can "do Turkey in 72 hours". For surgery, no — and any clinic that encourages it is cutting a corner with your healing. On the stay lengths published on this site:

  • Single implant or crowns: usually 5–7 nights for the first trip.
  • All-on-4 / All-on-6: a first trip of typically 5–7 days (extractions, implant placement, temporary teeth), then a second trip after 3–6 months of healing for the final bridge.
  • The hard floor: stay in Turkey at least 48–72 hours after surgery before flying. Cabin pressure and a long flight are not what a fresh surgical site needs, and the clinic needs a final in-person check before you leave.

What a short 72-hour trip can fit, in general, is the non-surgical end of the process: a consultation, 3D scans and a written treatment plan. The 72 hours below are the ones that start when you leave the clinic after surgery.

Day 0 — arrival day: set up the recovery before you need it

  • Flights: UK and Ireland to Turkey is a 2.5–4 hour flight, typically £100–£250 return — short-haul, no jet lag to speak of, which is one reason the corridor works.
  • Airport to hotel: Istanbul Airport (IST) is roughly 45–60 minutes to the Şişli clinic district in normal traffic, and 70–85 minutes at rush hour. Antalya Airport (AYT) is much closer to its clinic district — about 15–25 minutes by taxi or transfer to Lara/Şirinyalı. Book a clinic or hotel transfer for arrival; the metro or a bus with luggage is a false economy on a surgery trip.
  • Stock the room before surgery: buy the soft food, bottled water and any pharmacy items on arrival day, while you still feel fine. In Şişli, Cevahir Mall puts shops, pharmacies and food under one roof; in Antalya's clinic district, pharmacies line İsmet Gökçen Caddesi in Şirinyalı (typically open Mon–Sat 08:30–19:00).
  • Eat a proper meal the evening before surgery, and confirm your transfer times and the clinic's aftercare sheet before you sleep.

Hours 0–24 — surgery day: the only plan is rest

Surgery day itself is simple by design. You travel from the hotel to the clinic by transfer or taxi — in the clinic districts this is a 5–20 minute ride, not an expedition — have the procedure, and go straight back to the hotel. As general recovery guidance: expect the rest of the day to be written off. Rest in the room, keep to the medication schedule your clinic gives you, and do not plan anything for the evening, including dinner out.

Food: soft and cool only — soups, yoghurt, mashed foods, protein shakes. This is exactly why our Istanbul and Antalya hotel guides score 24-hour room service and a nearby supermarket above a famous breakfast buffet: at hour six, you want soup delivered, not a restaurant search.

Hours 24–48 — day two: check-up and short walks

Day two usually includes a short clinic visit — a check of the surgical site and, for All-on-4 patients, work on the temporary teeth. Keep the rest of the day light: as general guidance, short gentle walks near the hotel are normally fine and help you feel human again, but this is not the day for sightseeing. Istanbul's tourist districts (Sultanahmet, Taksim) add serious traffic time to every trip, and Antalya's Kaleiçi old town is 20–30 minutes from the Lara clinics — both can wait until a later trip, or not happen at all.

Food stays soft and cool. If your hotel has a kitchenette or your room has a fridge, day two is when reheated soup and yoghurt stop feeling like a compromise and start feeling like a system.

Hours 48–72 — the final check and flying home

  • The 48–72 hour mark is your earliest flying window — and only after the clinic's final in-person check. Most patients on a 5–7 night first trip fly home on day five, six or seven, comfortably past it.
  • Departure timing: do not book the first flight of the morning after your final check. Leave a buffer day between the last appointment and the flight in case the clinic wants one more look, and time the airport transfer for the traffic you will actually meet — up to 70–85 minutes to IST at rush hour, versus 15–25 minutes to AYT from Lara.
  • Before you leave: collect your paperwork, X-rays and implant details from the clinic, confirm what the second trip (if any) involves and when, and make sure you have the clinic's contact for questions once you are home.

What to avoid in the first 72 hours

  • Flying earlier than 48–72 hours after surgery — the single most common planning mistake on this corridor.
  • Treating the trip as a holiday: big sightseeing days, Bosphorus crossings for appointments, or a hotel in a tourist district across the city from your clinic. Stay in the clinic district — Şişli/Nişantaşı in Istanbul, Şirinyalı–Çağlayan in Antalya.
  • Hot, hard, spicy or crunchy food — as general recovery guidance, the first days are soft and cool foods only, and your clinic's instructions override everything here.
  • Smoking and alcohol — general recovery guidance is to avoid both while you heal; follow your clinic's specific advice.
  • Public transport with luggage after surgery — book the transfer. Clinic Expert's flat €270 VIP transfer add-on exists precisely because patients kept underestimating this.
  • A non-refundable hotel rate — clinic schedules move; pick the free-cancellation rate, as every guide on this site advises.

First-72-hours FAQ

Can I fly home the day after implant surgery in Turkey? No. Stay at least 48–72 hours after surgery before flying — that is why first trips for implants and All-on-4 run 5–7 nights, so the clinic can also do a final in-person check before you leave.

What can I eat in the first 72 hours? Soft, cool foods: soups, yoghurt, mashed foods and protein shakes. Stock them on arrival day, before surgery — afterwards you will want room service and a nearby supermarket, not a restaurant hunt.

Can I do a whole implant trip to Turkey in 72 hours? Not for surgery. On this site's published stay lengths, a single implant or crowns usually need 5–7 nights for the first trip, and All-on-4 is two trips with 3–6 months of healing between them. A 72-hour trip realistically fits a consultation, scans and treatment planning — the 72 hours in this guide are the recovery window after surgery, not the whole trip.

Who drives me between the airport, hotel and clinic? Most patients use clinic or hotel transfers rather than public transport with luggage after surgery. Clinic Expert, whose direct Turkey prices we publish, lists VIP airport and clinic transfers at €270 flat and a recovery hotel at €80/night as its standard add-ons, and many Turkish clinics bundle a hotel and transfers into their packages.

Istanbul or Antalya — does the recovery city matter? The pattern is the same in both: stay in the clinic district, rest for the first 72 hours, fly no earlier than 48–72 hours after surgery. In Istanbul that means Şişli/Nişantaşı; in Antalya, the Şirinyalı–Çağlayan clinic streets in Muratpaşa rather than the big resort strip.
