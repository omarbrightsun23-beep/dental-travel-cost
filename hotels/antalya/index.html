Where to Stay for Dental Work in Antalya: The Lara Clinic District

By the DentalTravelCost team · Prices checked November 2026

Antalya's dental clinics cluster in Muratpaşa, along the eastern Lara road — Şirinyalı and Çağlayan (the "Eski Lara" streets) are the residential clinic district, full of small hotels, cafés and pharmacies. Kaleiçi, the old town, is pretty and cheaper but sits 20–30 minutes west of the clinics. And out past Lara Beach, the Kundu strip is a row of giant all-inclusive resorts — wonderful for a holiday, wrong for the days after implant surgery. This guide covers the district first, then the old-town option, with hotel price bands we checked in November 2026.

How long do you need to stay?

  • Implants and All-on-4: Turkish clinics quote 5–7 nights for the first trip (surgery, checks, final fitting) and a second, shorter trip after 3–6 months of healing. Our calculator models 6 nights; most Antalya All-on-4 packages assume the same.
  • Crowns and veneers: usually 5–6 nights while the lab works.
  • After surgery: do not fly for at least 48–72 hours after extractions or implant placement — longer if your surgeon says so. Antalya Airport (AYT) is only 13–15 km from Lara: 15–25 minutes by taxi (about €13–14) or from about €35 for a private transfer, so the airport run is the easiest part of this trip.

The clinic district: Şirinyalı–Çağlayan (Lara side of Muratpaşa)

Stay here and your clinic is a short walk or a 5-minute taxi away, the streets are quiet and residential, and you have the one thing recovery actually needs on this coast: a pharmacy street. İsmet Gökşen Caddesi has several pharmacies within a few doors of each other (Kumbul at 69/A, Vitamin at 78/A, Pars at 79, Lotus at 62B — typically open Monday–Saturday 08:30–19:00), and Lara Park Eczanesi sits on Lara Caddesi at 101/B. Soft-food cafés and small supermarkets line the same streets.

Mid-range boutique, dental-patient focused — Der Inn Lara

€68–€159 per night (about £60–£140)

A small boutique hotel (32–36 rooms) in Şirinyalı that actively works with dental patients — clinic coordination and transfer support are part of its pitch, and it partners with a local clinic as a patient hotel. The recovery spec checks out: lift, soundproofed rooms, an on-site restaurant with room service, and the pharmacy street on its own doorstep. Book a rear or garden-facing room — front rooms get traffic noise from the main road, which reviewers mention. Refundable rates exist on some booking sites, while the cheapest rates are non-refundable — pick refundable if your clinic dates could shift. (The pound figure is our conversion estimate; the hotel and booking sites quote euros.)

Mid-range, 2026 caveat attached — Laren Seaside Hotel & Spa

Roughly £50–£140 / €65–€165 per night (peak season up to €197)

In Çağlayan, inside the clinic district, 40 rooms: lift, 24-hour room service, soundproofed rooms, sea glimpses from upper floors. Recent dental-patient reviews praise exactly the things that matter — staff helping with soft food, free water and milk during recovery. Read this before you book, though: 2026 reviews are mixed, and some guests reported run-down rooms and moved hotels mid-stay. If you choose it, book a renovated or deluxe room category, not the entry-level room, and confirm the room type with the hotel directly. Cancellation terms were not stated on the pages we checked, so assume the cheapest rate is not freely cancellable until the booking page proves otherwise.

Comfort — Akra Antalya

Roughly £122–£248 / €118–€247 per night

The full-service option in Şirinyalı: a 471-room five-star city hotel — not a party resort — with a lift, 24-hour room service, blackout curtains and a pillow menu, plus spa facilities for the days you feel human again. Reviews describe calm, quiet luxury; the counter-note is occasional late-night noise, so ask for a high-floor, sea-facing room away from the event spaces. Cancellation terms were not verified on the pages we checked — do not assume free cancellation; choose and read a refundable rate if you need one.

The old-town option: Kaleiçi

Kaleiçi is Antalya's walled old town — cobbled lanes, Ottoman houses, harbour views — and it is 11–13 km (20–30 minutes by taxi) from the Lara clinics. That is fine if your clinic provides transfers, which most Antalya clinics do, and fatal to your schedule if it doesn't. It is also a nightlife district: pick a quiet street and ask for a courtyard room.

Budget — Aspen Hotel Kaleiçi

Roughly £27–€41 / €32–€54 per night

A small hotel (41 rooms) with a lift, blackout drapes, 24-hour front desk, and food delivery to the room — and "quiet street view" room categories that suit light sleepers. The trade-offs are the distance above, small rooms by city-hotel standards, and Kaleiçi's evening noise if you land on a bar street. As part of a clinic package with transfers included, it is the best-value bed in this guide; without transfers, stay in Lara.

A warning about the Kundu / Lara Beach mega-resorts

The famous names east of Lara — the Titanic Deluxe Lara, Barut Lara and their neighbours — are superb holiday hotels and poor recovery hotels: waterparks, animation teams and nightlife, the longest taxi rides to the clinics, and buffet halls where soft food means whatever the buffet happens to hold. If you want a resort week, take it before your surgery or on the second, fitting-only trip — not in the 72 hours after implant placement. (The Falcon Hotel is the middle case: genuinely popular with dental patients, but still a large all-inclusive — fine if your clinic arranges everything, less fine if you are self-managing.)

Antalya recovery FAQ

Should I stay in Kaleiçi or Lara? Lara (Şirinyalı–Çağlayan) if you are booking your own hotel — it is the clinic district, with the pharmacies. Kaleiçi only if your clinic's package includes transfers and you accept 20–30 minutes each way.

Are Antalya clinics' hotel packages worth it? Often, yes — clinics negotiate rates and bundle transfers, and some work with specific partner hotels in the district. Compare the package against the bands in this guide; if it is at or under the band for the same district, take it and let them run the logistics.

What will I eat after surgery? In the clinic district: hotel room service (24-hour at Laren and Akra), café soups and soft breakfasts, and supermarket yoghurt and protein drinks on the pharmacy street. This is the practical advantage over a resort buffet.

How do I get from the airport? Taxi from about €13–14 or a pre-booked transfer from about €35 — 15–25 minutes to Lara. Book the return pickup with the same firm and give them your flight time, not your "ready" time.

Which hotels should I avoid after surgery? The Kundu mega-resorts (distance + noise + buffet food), and any cheap room whose selling point is the old-town nightlife. Recovery wants quiet, a lift, room service and a short clinic run — in that order.
