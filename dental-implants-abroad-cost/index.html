Why brand matters

The implant brand is a large part of the price difference. NucleOSS is the lowest-cost option in this list, SLH sits in the middle, and Straumann is the premium Swiss option. Ask which brand, crown material, and warranty documentation you will receive before comparing any quote — a lower headline price that omits the crown or uses an unbranded implant is not the same treatment.
