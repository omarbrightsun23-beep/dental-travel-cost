What to check before you compare quotes

  • Is the quote per arch or for both arches?
  • Does it include all 12 crowns, or only temporary teeth?
  • Which implant brand is used, and will you receive an implant passport with the brand and specifications?
  • Are scans, hotel, transfers, and any second trip included — or charged separately?
